    Recording mutual and automatic aid¶

    Every call where another department helped you, or you helped them, has to name that department on the NERIS record. LadderOps keeps a list of the departments you work with so you pick from it on the incident form instead of typing a NERIS ID each time.

    Set up your partners once¶

    Open Aid Agreements and choose Add nearby departments. LadderOps reads the NERIS directory for your county and lists every registered department, with the ones you already have marked. Untick any you do not run aid with, choose the agreement type (most rural partners are mutual aid), and add the rest in one step.

    To find a department in another county, change the NERIS ID prefix to that county, or search by name.

    Each partner can be edited later. Change the agreement type, add notes, or mark a partner inactive when an agreement ends. An inactive partner disappears from the incident picker but stays on the incidents where it was already recorded, so past reports do not change.

    On the incident¶

    In the incident's aid section, pick the partner from the list, say whether aid was given or received, and choose the aid type. The partner's NERIS ID comes along automatically. If a department that is not on your list helped, you can still type its name and NERIS ID for that one call.

    Fire departments are the only partners NERIS lets you record under an agreement. Other agencies on scene, such as EMS, law enforcement, or utilities, are recorded separately as non-fire agencies on the same section.

    If NERIS is not connected yet¶

    Adding nearby departments needs your department's NERIS credentials, because the directory is read through them. Until NERIS is connected, add partners by hand from the same page.
